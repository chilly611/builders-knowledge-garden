# Builder's Knowledge Garden — Where We're Headed
*A short brief for the team · 2026-06-02*

## The mission
Ship the Builder's Knowledge Garden killer app to general contractors as a real, paid product — now, not someday. One adaptive place that holds everything a GC carries in their head: the budget, the schedule, the sequencing, the codes, who's doing what, and what could go wrong. The system of record for the job.

## Why GCs, why now
A general contractor runs the whole job on memory, texts, and spreadsheets. When something slips through the cracks it costs them — in margin, in rework, in miscommunication, in lawsuits, in clients who expected one thing and got another. We put the whole job on one surface and make it the record everyone can trust. The payoff for the GC is simple: **make more money, get jobs done right the first time, head off the fights before they start, and keep plans and expectations realistic.** A dream for general contractors of every kind.

## How we open the door — Founding Builders
We're not waiting for perfect. Our first contractors come in as **Founding Builders** — trailblazers, brand ambassadors, and co-developers of the platform. We tell them honestly: it's early, and it's getting better fast. In return they get **special, immediate attention, a direct line to us, and a low price locked in for life.** Their real projects and real feedback steer what we build next. That honesty is the deal — and it's a good one.

## What's real, and what's next
We have a clear, working vision for the parts a GC will love today — the knowledge engine, the lifecycle surface, plain-language code answers cited to their source, and the project record. We also have an honest, specific list of what still has to get built to make this a true system of record. We're not pretending. We're inviting the right people to build it with us.

## How we move fast
Two fronts, one source of truth. **Chilly in San Diego and Michael in San Francisco, each paired with Claude,** running parallel agents across coding, research, design, and strategy. GitHub and Supabase are the single source of truth — what one of us does, the other sees. Three simple rules keep it clean: **divide the work so we never step on each other, write everything down, and prove it works by actually using it before we call it done.**

## The near-term goal
Cross from pre-revenue to our **first paying Founding Builders.** That crossing — real contractors paying for and living inside the product — is the gate we clear before we scale up investor conversations.

## The throughline
Transparency leads to harmony, harmony to happy clients, happy clients to fewer fights, fewer fights to more money — and ease and joy for everyone on the job. That's the whole story.

Let's ship.
