# First-run & onboarding — the OS pattern
Tier 2/3 practice for the first five minutes, reusable across gardens. Governed by
PLATFORM-CONSTITUTION.md (decisions 1 MLP · 2 the loop · 7 plain language · 13 convenience+control ·
18/20 visual-first · 19/21 flags — 20/21 are the canonical statements, 2026-06-10). Pairs with
docs/visual-first-and-flags.md.
## Why this exists
The 2026-06-08 founder dogfood pass confirmed the engine works — auth, context-routing across projects
and jurisdictions, the grounded AI take, persistence — and that the first five minutes did not yet earn
it. These are the principles the first-run is being rebuilt to. We re-house the working engine behind a
simpler front; we do not rebuild it.
## Thesis
One door, plain words, money-forward, role-aware — then get out of the way.
## Principles
1. One door. After the landing animation, exactly one obvious thing: a high-contrast "what do you want
   to build / get done?" field, with 3–5 concrete example chips so a blank box never stalls anyone. One
   input, consistent everywhere — not a pill plus a sparkle plus a compass plus cards all competing.
2. Plain words in the work surface. The product talks like a contractor; the poetry stays in marketing.
   No "Lens," no "RESTRICTED," no "A loved one." (Decision 7.)
3. Money and time up front. Lead the first real screen with the numbers and a few clearly differentiated
   options — Budget / Business Class / First-Class Luxury — with plain green/red signals. Not a wall of
   equal-weight cards. (Decisions 18, 19.)
4. Know who you're talking to, fast. One lightweight role read (owner vs contractor) sets language and
   lane: owners get outcomes and ROI; contractors get working docs and compliance. Same engine, two
   voices. Default: infer from what they type, confirm with one tap.
5. Earn the depth. Progressive Reveal — the dense cockpit is the expert view, not the first view. Show
   the one next step; reveal the seven stages and the full toolset as they go.
6. One thing is brightest on every screen. The herbarium palette stays, with real contrast steps. The
   center collage becomes a clean hero or steps back — coherent, not layered. (Decisions 4, 18.)
7. Re-house, don't rebuild. The grounded AI, persistence, multi-project, and multi-jurisdiction stay and
   move behind the simpler front. The engine is the moat; this is a surface pivot.
## Where it sits in the plan
The MLP bar (decision 1) biting: the loop runs but isn't lovable yet, so Phase 1's gate expands to include
the first-run experience. Two tracks, non-colliding — (A) plumbing: the localhost crash (P0), the
persistence gap, LLM determinism + generated-image caching; (B) the design-led first-run rebuild, Design →
Code as the next write-lane.
Implementing design (added 2026-06-10): the Claude Design spec **"First Run — The First Five Minutes"**
is the implementing design for these seven principles.
