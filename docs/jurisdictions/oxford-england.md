## 3. Permit authority & process (two separate consents)

**(a) Building Regulations approval — Building Control.** Oxford City Council operates a Building Control service; applicants may alternatively use a private **Registered Building Control Approver** (the post-Building-Safety-Act successor to "Approved Inspector"). Full Plans / Building Notice routes; ends in a Completion Certificate. 🟡

**(b) Planning permission — Local Planning Authority.** Oxford City Council is the LPA; applications via its Public Access portal. 🟡

**(c) Higher-risk buildings** (broadly ≥18 m or ≥7 storeys with ≥2 dwellings): the **Building Safety Regulator** is the sole building control body for design/construction/occupation. As of a **27 Jan 2026** change, BSR is a standalone executive non-departmental public body sponsored by MHCLG (previously within HSE). 🟡

## 5. Local legal review required

- **Building Control sign-off** (Oxford City Council or Registered Building Control Approver) → Completion Certificate. 🟡
- **Planning permission** from Oxford City Council (LPA). 🟡
- **Oxford-specific heritage controls — material and criminal-liability bearing:**
  - Oxford has **18 Conservation Areas** (incl. the Central City & University area; appraisal adopted 15 Nov 2023 — a material planning consideration). 🟡
  - **Listed Building Consent** is required for works (internal or external) affecting a listed building's character; the Council warns unauthorised work "is an offence … maximum penalty could include imprisonment and unlimited fines." 🟡
- **Building Safety Act 2022 duties** for higher-risk buildings (engage BSR). 🟡
- **Site-specific heritage search is mandatory before design:** confirm Conservation-Area status, listing grade (National Heritage List for England / Historic England), and any **Article 4 Directions** removing permitted-development rights. 🔴
