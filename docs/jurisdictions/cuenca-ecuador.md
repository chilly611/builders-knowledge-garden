## 2. Adopted code(s) + edition/year

**Norma Ecuatoriana de la Construcción (NEC)** — nationally mandatory; supersedes the former Código Ecuatoriano de la Construcción. Structured in three axes — **NEC-SE** (Seguridad Estructural), **NEC-HS** (Habitabilidad y Salud), **NEC-SB** (Servicios Básicos). 🟡

Chapters named on the official MIDUVI FAQ (fetched in research pass): NEC-SE-CG (loads), **NEC-SE-DS (seismic hazard / seismic-resistant design)**, NEC-SE-RE, NEC-SE-GC (geotechnics/foundations), NEC-SE-HM (reinforced concrete), NEC-SE-MP (masonry), NEC-SE-AC (steel), NEC-SE-MD (timber), NEC-SE-VIVIENDA, NEC-SE-GUADÚA, NEC-HS-AU (universal accessibility), NEC-HS-CI (fire), NEC-HS-VIDRIO (glass). 🟡

**Adoption / editions:** the initial six NEC-SE chapters were adopted by **Acuerdo Ministerial No. 0028 (19 Aug 2014)**, published in **Registro Oficial No. 319, 2º Suplemento, 26 Aug 2014**; later chapters (e.g., NEC-HS-AU, NEC-SE-GUADÚA) via **A.M. 033-16**, **Registro Oficial No. 842 (2017)**. 🟡🔴 *(adoption dates confirmed via official FAQ + search; the Registro-Oficial PDFs and the per-chapter current edition could not be read from the MIDUVI site — see gaps)*

**Seismic note:** Ecuador is a high-seismic country; **NEC-SE-DS** compliance is non-negotiable (Cuenca sits in the Andean seismic zone). 🟡

## 3. Permit authority & process

Two parallel municipal tracks (GAD Cuenca):

- **Permiso de Intervención Mayor** — issued by the **Dirección General de Control Municipal** for works > 50 m² (outside the heritage area). 🟡
- **Permiso de Construcción Mayor** — issued by the **Dirección de Áreas Históricas y Patrimoniales** for properties within the historic/heritage area (confirmed first-hand: requires an approved architectural plan, a fiel-cumplimiento guarantee lodged with Tesorería Municipal; counters at Presidente Córdova/Padre Aguirre). ✅
- **Prerequisite:** *Aprobación de Proyectos Arquitectónicos*, which itself requires the **IPRUS** (land-use clearance) + architectural drawings/studies. 🟡
- Online: enlinea.cuenca.gob.ec; GIS at ide.cuenca.gob.ec. ✅(portal links on cuenca.gob.ec)

**Local land-use instrument:** *Ordenanza de control para el suelo urbano, suelo rural y suelo rural de expansión urbana del cantón Cuenca* (pub. 31 May 2021); **PDOT/PUGS 2025** is the current territorial plan. 🟡

## 5. Local legal review required

- **Designs signed by an Ecuadorian-registered architect/engineer** (SENESCYT-registered + professional association). ⚠️ *(general professional-practice; confirm)*
- **GAD Cuenca approval chain:** IPRUS → Aprobación de Proyectos Arquitectónicos → Permiso de Intervención Mayor (Control Municipal) **or** Permiso de Construcción Mayor (Áreas Históricas). ✅/🟡
- **NEC compliance** — nationally mandatory; **NEC-SE-DS (seismic)** especially. 🟡
- **Heritage controls** — Cuenca's historic centre is UNESCO-listed (1999); interventions in the heritage area go through the Dirección de Áreas Históricas y Patrimoniales under the 2010 (and 2024 PPEAHP) ordinances. ✅/🟡🔴
- **Technical studies** (geotechnical/NEC-SE-GC, structural, fire, accessibility) per project type. 🟡
