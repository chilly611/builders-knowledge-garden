## 3. Permit authority & process (jurisdiction-dependent)

- **Mainland Dubai:** **Dubai Municipality, BRPA — Building Permits Department**; e-services at hub.dm.gov.ae. Engineering-consultancy practice now regulated by **Law No. (14) of 2025**; contracting by **Law No. (7) of 2025**. 🟡
- **Trakhees / PCFC free zones** (Dubai World / Nakheel / DMCC / Dubai Maritime City): Trakhees–Civil Engineering Dept issues permits (ePermit), implementing the DBC per Decree 45/2021 (Circular CEDGR-135, 20 Dec 2021). 🟡
- **DDA (TECOM-group free zones:** Internet/Media/Knowledge/Design districts): DDA issues building/piling/sub-structure/excavation/modification permits via its Zoning e-Services. 🟡
- **Dubai Science Park (DSOA):** named in the DBC as a co-authority. 🔴 *(its own permit page not fetched)*
- **Dubai Civil Defence (DCD):** fire & life-safety NOC/approval required across all jurisdictions (incl. Hassantuk alarm monitoring). 🟡

## 5. Local legal review required

- **DM-registered engineering consultant** must prepare/stamp designs (Law No. (14) of 2025); Trakhees/DDA accreditation for their zones. 🟡
- **Building permit** from the authority with jurisdiction (DM / Trakhees / DDA) — **determine jurisdiction by parcel first**. 🟡
- **Dubai Civil Defence approval** (fire & life safety; Hassantuk connection). 🟡
- **Construction insurance** (Executive Council Resolution No. (28) of 2022). 🟡
- **Professional Arabic legal translation + review of Decree No. (45) of 2021** before relying on code scope/application. 🔴
- Sector NOCs as applicable (DEWA, telecoms, DHA, etc.). ⚠️
